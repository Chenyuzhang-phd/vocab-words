故事一：开学第一天

First Day of School
主题：校园生活 | 高亮颜色：■ 黄色
Li Hua is an ardent(热情的) student and the incumbent(现任的) class president, making her classroom the hub(中心) of school activities. She always carries a portfolio(文件夹) of notes and a brochure(小册子) about clubs. At first she worried the new teacher would be arrogant(傲慢的), but that was just a stereotype(刻板印象) — he said arithmetic(算术) is the staple(主要的) foundation of math and repetition(重复) is the best way to learn. During break, she bought stationery(文具) and saw older students intimidate(恐吓) a younger boy, which she thought was shameful(可耻的) and an insult(侮辱) to the school spirit. Her courage was integral(不可或缺的) to school safety, and with each increment(增加) of confidence she grew as a leader. The teacher's wisdom would baffle(使困惑)d her at first, but his kindness was no mere semblance(表象). She used her intelligence(智力) to give an incisive(精辟的) speech at the class meeting.
李华是一个热情的学生，也是现任班长，她的教室是学校活动的中心。她总是带着一个文件夹和一本关于社团的小册子。起初她担心新老师会很傲慢，但这只是一种刻板印象——他说算术是数学的主要基础，重复是最好的学习方法。课间时，她去买文具，看到高年级学生恐吓一个低年级男孩，她觉得这是可耻的，是对学校精神的侮辱。她的勇气对校园安全是不可或缺的，随着信心的增加她成长为更好的领导者。老师的智慧一开始让她困惑，但他的善良不只是表象。她用自己的智力在班会上做了精辟的发言。

故事二：周末大扫除

The Weekend Cleanup
主题：家庭日常 | 高亮颜色：■ 绿色
Saturday morning was always hectic(忙乱的) at Zhang's home — cleanup day! Dad was steadfast(坚定的) and believed good sanitation(卫生) was important for the family. Mom filled a bucket to the brim(边缘) with soapy water and used a garden hose(软管) to wash windows. Xiao Mei found old starch(淀粉) powder, threw it in the trash(垃圾), and tried to squash(压扁) it down, but the brittle(易碎的) bag almost broke. The sink had stagnant(停滞的) water that smelled bad, and the air felt static(静态的) before she opened the window. Xiao Gang cleaned the bathroom until everything was sterile(无菌的), removing hair that started to tangle(缠结) in the drain. He showed great vigilance(警惕) checking every corner, then decided to brew(冲泡) tea for everyone. The family kept the kitchen sanitary(卫生的) so food would not perish(腐烂) easily.
星期六早上在张家总是忙乱的——今天是大扫除日！爸爸很坚定，相信良好的卫生对家庭很重要。妈妈把水桶装到边缘装满肥皂水，用花园软管冲洗窗户。小梅发现一袋过期的淀粉，扔进垃圾里，试着把它压扁，但易碎的垃圾袋差点破掉。水槽里有发臭的停滞的水，打开窗户前空气感觉是静态的。小刚打扫浴室直到一切都无菌，清除了排水口开始缠结的头发。他表现出极大的警惕检查每个角落，然后决定为大家冲泡一杯茶。一家人保持厨房卫生，这样食物就不容易腐烂。

故事三：第一份实习

My First Internship
主题：职场新人 | 高亮颜色：■ 蓝色
As a novice(新手) intern, Xiao Wang was ready to commence(开始) his inaugural(首次的) day at work. The company offered a generous incentive(激励) of bonuses and free meals, and from his window he could see the marine(海洋的) bay. The company passed new legislation(法规) about flexible hours, and his boss's inference(推断) was that Xiao Wang had legitimate(合理的) talent for creative work. Sometimes he had insomnia(失眠) from excitement, and his obsession(痴迷) with learning kept him up late. He found his own niche(合适的位置) and loved the linen(亚麻的) curtains and lime(酸橙) green walls. At lunch, he visited a nearby zoo and saw a kangaroo(袋鼠) and an ostrich(鸵鸟). The lavish(奢华的) gift shop had a leopard(豹子) plushie. There was a public outcry(强烈抗议) about the zoo being too small, but the benefits outweigh(超过) the costs. The cafe served palatable(可口的) sandwiches and bone marrow(骨髓) soup. An old observatory(天文台) on a hill seemed to loom(赫然耸现) over the path. The guide gave a lucid(清晰的) explanation of telescopes, and he learned that volunteers are like martyr(烈士)s, while the zoo follows linear(线性的) welfare rules.
作为一名新手实习生，小王准备好开始他的首次工作日了。公司提供丰厚的激励——奖金和免费餐食，从靠窗的办公桌他可以看到海洋的海湾。公司通过了关于弹性工作时间的新法规，老板的推断是小王在创意工作方面有合理的天赋。有时他因为兴奋而失眠，对学习的痴迷让他熬夜。他找到了自己合适的位置，喜欢亚麻的窗帘和酸橙绿的墙壁。午休时，他去了附近的动物园，看到了一只袋鼠和一只鸵鸟。奢华的礼品店有一只豹子毛绒玩具。公众对动物园太小表示强烈抗议，但好处超过了成本。咖啡馆供应可口的三明治和骨髓汤。山上的古老天文台似乎赫然耸现在小路上。向导清晰地解释了望远镜，他了解到志愿者就像烈士，动物园遵循线性的福利规则。

故事四：郊外徒步

A Hike in the Countryside
主题：周末出游 | 高亮颜色：■ 紫色
Last weekend, Xiao Lin and her friends went hiking in the countryside. They saw many aquatic(水生的) plants by a beautiful lake and spotted arboreal(树栖的) squirrels jumping in the trees. A guide said a carnivore(食肉动物) like a fox lives in these woods. Most animals here are not nocturnal(夜间活动的) — they are active during the day. Every predator(捕食者) plays an important role in nature, while rabbits and deer are common prey(猎物) for larger animals. They also saw a small reptile(爬行动物) — a lizard sunning on a rock. A mouse is a type of rodent(啮齿动物) that lives in fields, and a crow is a scavenger(食腐动物) that eats whatever it finds. The dense vegetation(植被) in the forest provides homes and food for all these creatures.
上周末，小林和朋友们去郊外徒步。他们看到一个美丽的湖边生长着许多水生的植物，还发现树栖的松鼠在树上跳来跳去。一位向导说，像狐狸这样的食肉动物生活在这片树林里。这里的大多数动物都不是夜间活动的——它们白天活动。每一个捕食者都在自然界中扮演着重要的角色，而兔子和鹿是大型动物常见的猎物。他们还看到了一只小型爬行动物——一只在岩石上晒太阳的蜥蜴。老鼠是一种生活在田野里的啮齿动物，乌鸦是一种食腐动物，吃它能找到的任何东西。森林里茂密的植被为所有这些生物提供了家园和食物。

故事五：超市大采购

A Day at the Supermarket
主题：购物日记 | 高亮颜色：■ 橙色
On Saturday, Xiao Li went to the supermarket where ads would allude(暗示) to great deals as she liked to amble(漫步) through the aisles, spotting cookies Aloft(在高处) on the top shelf. The alpine(高山的) yogurt came from a farm at high altitude(海拔), and the amenable(通情达理的) manager greeted everyone warmly. An old lady with amnesia(健忘症) who was bilingual(双语的) asked for help, and the fruit section had amber(琥珀色的) peaches and aluminum(铝制的) cans. An ambiguous(模棱两可的) price label was a blunder(错误) by the staff, and she felt ambivalent(矛盾的) about buying snacks. The bread section sold analogue(类似物)s of traditional cakes, and candy came in amorphous(无定形的) shapes that were truly bizarre(奇异的). She liked studying the anatomy(构造) of fruits, heard anecdotal(传闻的) ghost stories, and saw a colossal(巨大的) display of bleach(漂白剂). She bought a new blouse(女式衬衫), the scale needed calibration(校准), and outside a vendor(小贩) sold flowers while a pedestrian(行人) with a pendant(吊坠) walked by. Stealing is a sin(罪过), and a sinister(阴险的) man made her uneasy. People say the old town was once full of anarchy(无政府状态), the fire alarm sounded like a siren(警报器), and without food people would die of starvation(饥饿). The store had high stature(声望) and followed every local statute(法规); long lines could torment(折磨) shoppers but self-checkout helps unravel(解决) the problem. A proponent(支持者) of healthy eating stood by the vegetables, the store had to purge(清除) old food weekly, and the jewelry counter had beautiful quartz(石英) stones. Theft was rampant(猖獗的) last year but cameras helped rectify(纠正) it, and during the economic upheaval(动荡) the store changed a lot. Fresh varnish(清漆) made floors shine, the manager worked to vindicate(证明...正确) his price cuts, and the pet section had books about viper(毒蛇) snakes. Business was at its zenith(顶峰) during holidays; the owner was a maverick(特立独行者) whose favorite maxim(格言) was "the customer is always right." Monthly Mediation(调解) occurred between staff and management, the history section had medieval(中世纪的) books, and some customers liked to meditate(冥想) in the quiet corner. Staff would nominate(提名) the best worker each month, the obstinate(固执的) bakery chef never changed recipes, and the flower shop had venomous(有毒的)-looking plants that were harmless.
星期六，小李去超市购物，广告会暗示有超值优惠，她喜欢在过道里漫步，在高处的顶层货架上发现了饼干。高山酸奶来自高海拔的农场，通情达理的店长热情地迎接顾客。一位患健忘症的双语老太太请求帮助，水果区有琥珀色的桃子和铝制的果汁罐。模棱两可的价格标签是员工的错误，她对买零食感到矛盾。面包区出售传统糕点的类似物，糖果有无定形的形状，看起来真的很奇异。她喜欢研究水果的构造，听到过传闻的鬼故事，还看到了一个巨大的漂白剂展示架。她买了一件新的女式衬衫，秤需要校准，外面一个小贩在卖花，一个戴着吊坠的行人走过。偷窃是罪过，一个阴险的男人让她不安。人们说老城区曾经充满无政府状态，火警听起来像警报器，没有食物人们会死于饥饿。这家店声望很高，遵守每项当地法规；长队会折磨购物者但自助结账有助于解决问题。一位健康饮食的支持者站在蔬菜旁，商店每周清除过期食品，珠宝柜台有美丽的石英。去年盗窃猖獗但摄像头帮助纠正了情况，经济动荡期间商店变化很大。新的清漆让地板闪亮，经理努力证明降价是正确的，宠物区有关于毒蛇的书。节日期间生意处于顶峰；店主是个特立独行者，最喜欢的格言是"顾客永远是对的"。每月员工和管理层之间有调解，历史区有中世纪的书，一些顾客喜欢在安静的角落冥想。员工每月提名最佳员工，固执的面包师从不改变食谱，花店有看起来有毒但其实无害的植物。

故事六：学做蛋糕

Learning to Bake a Cake
主题：美食厨房 | 高亮颜色：■ 青色
Xiao Mei decided to learn baking at a cooking class. She mixed the batter(面糊) carefully until smooth, and the sweet smell seemed to beckon(召唤) her to the kitchen. She promised not to betray(背叛) the secret recipe, as they had a bilateral(双边的) agreement — no sharing with outsiders. The oven was hot with a small blaze(火焰) visible through the glass, and outside the weather was bleak(荒凉的) and rainy. She walked with brisk(轻快的) steps to fetch ingredients, and the cookie edges were brittle(易碎的) and crispy. A bulletin(公告) board showed class schedules, and she felt a small bump(碰撞) on her arm from carrying heavy bags. After class, she ate at the school cafeteria(自助餐厅), where the meal had plenty of calcium(钙) from milk and cheese. She counted every calorie(卡路里) to stay healthy, and for dessert she had cane(甘蔗) sugar candy. The teacher gave her a vitamin capsule(胶囊) for energy, and said the cardinal(主要的) rule of baking is: always follow the recipe. A mother's gentle caress(爱抚) can calm a crying child, and Xiao Mei learned to carve(雕刻) beautiful patterns on cake frosting. The school was near a hotel with a casino(赌场), and there was a small casualty(事故) in class — someone burned their finger. The teacher said curiosity is a catalyst(催化剂) for learning, older people sometimes develop cataract(白内障) and need surgery, and you must cease(停止) cooking at the right time. A famous celebrity(名人) chef visited last month, the kitchen floor was made of cement(水泥), and on her way home she passed an old cemetery(墓地) with flowers. The government does a census(人口普查) every ten years, she bought ceramic(陶瓷的) bowls, ate cereal(麦片) for breakfast, and the school held a ceremonial(仪式的) graduation.
小美决定在烹饪班学烘焙。她仔细搅拌面糊直到顺滑，甜美的香味似乎在召唤她走进厨房。她答应不背叛老师分享的秘密食谱，因为他们有双边的协议——不向外人透露。烤箱很热，透过玻璃可以看到一小簇火焰，外面天气荒凉又下雨。她迈着轻快的步子去拿食材，饼干边缘易碎又酥脆。公告板上展示着课程表，她因提重物感到手臂上有一个小碰撞肿块。下课后她在学校自助餐厅吃饭，这顿饭从牛奶和奶酪中获得了充足的钙。她计算每一卡路里来保持健康，甜点吃了甘蔗糖做的糖果。老师给了她一粒维生素胶囊补充能量，说烘焙的主要规则是：永远按食谱来。母亲温柔的爱抚能让哭泣的孩子平静，小美学会了在蛋糕糖霜上雕刻美丽图案。学校附近有一家带赌场的酒店，课堂上发生了一起小事故——有人烫伤了手指。老师说好奇心是学习新技能的催化剂，老年人有时会患白内障需要手术，你必须在合适的时间停止烹饪。上个月一位著名的名人厨师来访，厨房地板是水泥做的，回家路上她经过一个古老的墓地。政府每十年做一次人口普查，她买了陶瓷的碗，早餐吃了麦片，学校举行了仪式的毕业典礼。

故事七：健身房的一天

A Day at the Gym
主题：健康生活 | 高亮颜色：■ 红色
Xiao Chen joined a gym to get in shape, and today was his first day. He was agile(敏捷的) and moved quickly between machines, but after running he felt the agony(痛苦) of sore muscles. The gym felt alien(外星人/陌生的) at first, and he had an allergy(过敏) to the cleaning products. A trainer helped him ascertain(查明) his fitness level, but all the equipment bewilder(使困惑)ed him. He read a health column(专栏) by a famous columnist(专栏作家), and the gym hung a poster to commemorate(纪念) its anniversary. The TV played sports commentary(评论) while people exercised, and the trainer gave definitive(确定的) advice. He drank water so he would not dehydrate(脱水), and the humble trainer would never deign(屈尊) to look down on beginners. The gym had a program to prevent youth delinquency(不良行为), and after heavy rain there was a deluge(洪水) in the parking lot. The gym studied demographic(人口的) data to understand members, and he learned to detach(分开) his mind from tiredness. He faced a dilemma(困境) — lift more weights or do more cardio? The joy of finishing is ephemeral(短暂的), and small injuries can escalate(升级) into big problems. He asked a staff member to fetch(取来) him a towel, a snow flake(薄片) drifted past the window, and he heard about a membership fraud(欺诈) scam. The gym offered physical rehabilitation(康复) programs, the trainer was relentless(无情的) and never let anyone give up, and after a swim his hair was saturate(浸透)d with water. The gym used semiconductor(半导体) technology in smart equipment, public sentiment(情绪) was very positive, and he saw a man tumble(跌倒) on the treadmill. The gym warned about ultraviolet(紫外线的) rays from tanning beds, he was unaffected(不受影响的) by the loud music, and efficient utilisation(利用) of equipment saved time. He treated every workout with utmost(极度的) care, it was utter(完全的) silence in the yoga room, and the water fountain had a special valve(阀门). After exercise, tiredness seemed to vanish(消失), and he was determined to vanquish(征服) his bad habits. He bought velvet(天鹅绒) yoga pants, his friend sought vengeance(复仇) on the scale by losing weight, and the piano teacher at the community center was a virtuoso(大师). The gym was an affiliate(附属) of a national chain, he gave himself words of affirmation(肯定) every morning, and most members came from affluent(富裕的) neighborhoods. The gym was like a beacon(灯塔) of health, and on his way out he dropped a bead(珠子) from his bracelet but found it quickly.
小陈加入健身房来健身，今天是他第一天。他很敏捷，在器械之间快速移动，但跑步后感到肌肉酸痛的痛苦。健身房一开始很陌生，他对清洁产品过敏。教练帮他查明健身水平，但所有器械都让他困惑。他读了一位著名专栏作家写的健康专栏，健身房挂了海报纪念周年。电视播放着体育评论，教练给出确定的建议。他喝水以免脱水，谦虚的教练绝不会屈尊看不起初学者。健身房有预防青少年不良行为的项目，大雨后停车场有洪水。健身房研究人口数据来了解会员，他学会把思想从疲惫中分开。他面临困境——多举重还是多做有氧？完成的喜悦是短暂的，小伤可能升级成大问题。他让工作人员取来毛巾，一片雪花飘过窗外，他听说了会员欺诈骗局。健身房提供物理康复项目，教练很无情从不让任何人放弃，游泳后头发被水浸透。健身房在智能设备中使用半导体技术，公众情绪非常积极，他看到一个男人在跑步机上跌倒。健身房警告日光浴床发出紫外线，他不受嘈杂音乐影响，高效利用器械节省时间。他以极度认真对待每次锻炼，瑜伽室里是完全的寂静，饮水机有特殊阀门。运动后疲惫似乎消失了，他决心征服坏习惯。他买了天鹅绒瑜伽裤，朋友通过减重来向体重秤复仇，社区中心钢琴老师是大师。健身房是全国连锁的附属店，他每天早上给自己肯定的话语，大多数会员来自富裕的社区。健身房像健康的灯塔，出门时他从手链上掉了一颗珠子但很快找到了。

故事八：智能家居

My Smart Home
主题：科技日常 | 高亮颜色：■ 青蓝色
Xiao Zhao just moved into a new smart home full of amazing technology. Every room had a clever gadget(小器械) that made life easier, and he walked with a confident gait(步态) proud of his new home. His smart speaker could tell you about every galaxy(星系) in space, and he had the gall(胆量) to try every feature on the first day. He even wore futuristic garb(装束) — a smart watch and wireless earbuds, and the kitchen counter was made of granite(花岗岩). The system was built from grass-roots(基层的) ideas from ordinary people, and a grid(网格) of sensors covered every room. The door hinge(铰链) was automatic, smart devices continue to proliferate(激增), and the AI assistant could quell(平息) your worries by answering questions. A cold drink from the smart fridge helped quench(解渴) his thirst, and all devices came with a refundable(可退款的) warranty. You can refute(反驳) the idea that smart homes are unsafe, and Regardless(不管) of how busy you are, a smart home saves time. A heap(堆) of packages arrived on moving day, and movers had to heave(用力举起) the heavy sofa up the stairs. Making indecent(不得体的) jokes near the smart speaker is not polite, the main artery(动脉/要道) of the house was the hallway, and the smart TV played hilarious(欢闹的) shows. There was an indefinite(不确定的) delay in internet service, home insurance provides indemnity(赔偿) if something gets damaged, and a full battery is indicative(预示的) of a well-charged device. 在城市法院里，李法官已经习惯了处理棘手的案件。今天，一个年轻人被指控犯有盗窃罪，陪审团认真倾听律师试图为他的当事人辩护。正当理由是他既绝望又贫困，法官请裁判检查证据。这个人的律师是一位精通业务的专业人士，拥有极高的专门技术，法律这个职业需要诚实。法律的基本前提是公平，在初步听证中法官必须公正地主持。法院的声望取决于每位法官都享有声望且值得信赖，内部规则很严格。律师之间的通信必须透明，如果合同被判为无效的就没有法律效力。这个人的公然行为被摄像头拍到了——他在饥饿时无法克制自己不去偷窃。法官必须深入调查，发现这个人被家人抛弃了——这是他坎坷人生的前兆。很容易预言，如果没有帮助他会继续走上一条不好的道路。前缀"pre-"意思是"在……之前"——比如"predict"意思是提前说出来，这个人的女朋友怀孕了增加了压力，法官小心翼翼地向法庭做了陈述。他认为优先考虑的是改造而非惩罚。规定是社区服务和职业培训，法官赞同用替代判决，这个人的旧签证很快要到期了。政府会资助他的培训，二次机会的学说指引着法官，他给年轻人忠告：改变你的人生。这个人试图反驳但法官很坚定，他想要根本性的改变。法院会批准这个计划，在连续的几个月里这个人要向法院报到。如果他完成了计划就没有人会起诉他。这个人正处于人生的中间阶段，城市中的腐败是一个随之发生的问题，但正义要从一件案件一件案件做起。

故事十四：探索与冒险

Exploration and Adventure
主题：探索与发现 | 高亮颜色：■ 品红
A group of explorers set out to find an authentic(真实的) ancient city deep in the jungle. The forest did not abound(富于/充满) with clear paths, and their exploration(探险) had just begun. An abrupt(突然的) storm surprised everyone, the guide was furious(狂怒的) and made a big fuss(大惊小怪) about the delay, but the team's motivation(动机/动力) was strong. They needed fuel(燃料) for their lamps and kept walking through the bustle(喧嚣) of the noisy forest. After the storm, they found a strange cradle(摇篮/发源地) of civilization — ancient stone buildings covered in mould(霉菌). The countless(无数的) carvings on the walls told stories of the past, and a mortal(必死的/人类的) king had ruled here long ago. The carvings showed his conquest(征服) of nearby lands, and the hectare(公顷) of land was once a glorious(辉煌的) kingdom. The temple had exquisite(精致的) paintings, like stepping into a fiction(小说) story. They found an ancient idiom(成语/习惯用语) carved in stone, a hymn(赞美诗) was written on the wall, and the costume(服装)s of the ancient people were depicted in the art. They discovered a potent(强有力的) medicine plant — its smell entered their nostril(鼻孔)s with a sharp scent, and it could heal(治愈) many diseases. One explorer had a limp(跛行) from an old injury, and after applying the plant his pain began to subside(减退/平息). They would linger(逗留) here for days studying the ruins, the motion(运动) of the river provided fresh water, and they made sure to retain(保留) samples of everything. An outburst(爆发) of joy came when they discovered a golden mask, and they stayed overnight(通宵的/过夜的) in the ruins. The minimum(最小值/最小的) supplies they had were enough, they kept retail(零售) supplies in their backpacks, and their lengthy(冗长的) journey was paying off. A nearby lighthouse(灯塔) guided them back to the coast, and the grant(拨款/授予) from the university funded their trip. The university would consign(交付/委托) the artifacts to a museum, they did not want to hurl(猛投/猛掷) away their findings carelessly, and time would corrode(腐蚀) the ancient stones if not protected. The team would resume(重新开始) the expedition next year, some members would retire(退休) from fieldwork after this trip, and they would crawl(爬行) back through the jungle. The discovery was a tremendous(巨大的) achievement, they felt vigorous(精力旺盛的) and alive, and their story was superb(卓越的) — one for the history books.
一群探险家出发去寻找丛林深处一座真实的古城。森林里并不富于清晰的路径，他们的探险才刚刚开始。突然的暴风雨让所有人吃惊，向导很狂怒，为延误大惊小怪，但团队的动力很足。他们需要燃料来点灯，继续穿过喧嚣的丛林。暴风雨后，他们发现了一个奇特的文明摇篮——布满霉菌的古老石头建筑。墙上无数的雕刻讲述了过去的故事，一位必死的国王很久以前曾统治这里。雕刻展示了他对附近土地的征服，这片公顷的土地曾经是辉煌的王国。寺庙里有精致的绘画，就像走进了小说故事。他们发现了一块石头上刻着的古代成语，墙上写着一首赞美诗，古代人的服装也在艺术中被描绘。他们发现了一种强有力的药用植物——气味进入鼻孔带着刺鼻的香味，它可以治愈许多疾病。一位探险家因旧伤而跛行，敷上植物后疼痛开始减退。他们会在这里逗留几天研究废墟，附近河流的运动提供了淡水，他们确保保留所发现的一切样本。当他们发现一个金色面具时爆发出欢乐的欢呼，他们在废墟中过了一夜。他们携带的最少量的物资就足够了，背包里存放着零售物资，冗长的旅程终于有了回报。附近的一座灯塔指引他们回到海岸，大学提供的拨款资助了这次旅行。大学会将这些文物交付给博物馆，他们不想不小心地猛投丢掉发现，如果不加以保护时间会腐蚀古老的石头。团队明年会重新开始探险，有些成员旅行后将退出野外工作，他们会爬行穿过丛林到达海岸。这次发现是巨大的成就，他们感到精力旺盛而充满活力，他们的故事是卓越的——足以载入史册。

故事十五：日常生活百态

Scenes from Daily Life
主题：日常与情感 | 高亮颜色：■ 海蓝
In the suburb(郊区) where Xiao Li lived, life was simple. Every morning she would activate(激活) her phone and drink water to quench her thirst(口渴). The rhythm(节奏) of daily life was calm, she wore a ribbon(丝带) in her hair, and her room had tile(瓷砖) floors and a transparent(透明的) glass window. On her desk was a tray(托盘) holding her treasure(珍宝/珍爱)s — small gifts from friends. She was apt(有…倾向的/灵巧的) at drawing, her cat would curl(卷曲) up beside her, and sometimes the cat would doze(打瞌睡) on her lap. She felt the virtue(德行/优点) of patience was important in life. One day, there was a water shortage(不足/缺少) in the neighborhood, a trench(沟渠) was dug in the street, and the city had to summon(召集) workers quickly. She watched the workers tighten(拧紧) the bolts on the new pipes and did not despise(鄙视) their hard work — she respected it. The workers' velocity(速度) was impressive, a supersonic(超音速的) jet flew overhead, and a coffee stain(污点) on her shirt made her frown(皱眉). She felt a shiver(发抖/冷颤) in the cold wind, her neighbor was rigid(死板的/刚硬的) in his opinions, and the rim(边缘) of her cup was chipped. She tried to heal(治愈) her chapped hands with cream. Xiao Li's uncle had a donkey(驴) on his farm and ran a small dock(码头) by the river. At the dock(码头), boats brought fresh fish, and the approximate(近似的/大约的) number of fish per day was about two hundred. Her uncle was a venerable(值得尊敬的) old man with a vigorous(精力旺盛的) spirit despite his age. Sometimes people would threaten(威胁) to take his land, but he never gave in and did not seek revenge(报仇/复仇) — he believed in peace. His life was superb(卓越的) and full of meaning, and he showed tremendous(巨大的) courage in hard times.
在小李居住的郊区，生活很简单。每天早上她激活手机查看消息，喝水解口渴。日常生活的节奏平静，她在头发上系了一条丝带，房间有瓷砖地板和透明的玻璃窗。桌上有一个托盘放着她的珍宝——朋友们送的小礼物。她擅长画画，猫会卷曲在她身旁，有时猫会在她腿上打瞌睡。她觉得耐心这个德行在生活中很重要。有一天，附近出现了水不足的情况，街上挖了一条沟渠，城市不得不迅速召集工人。她看着工人们拧紧新管道上的螺栓，她不鄙视他们的辛苦工作——她很尊重。工人们的速度令人赞叹，一架超音速的喷气式飞机从头顶飞过，衬衫上的咖啡污点让她皱眉。她在冷风中发抖，邻居在观点上很死板，她杯子的边缘磕破了。她试着用护手霜治愈干裂的手。小李的叔叔在农场里养了一头驴，还在河边经营一个小码头。在码头，船只带来了新鲜的鱼，每天近似的捕鱼数量大约是两百条。她的叔叔是一位值得尊敬的老人，尽管年事已高仍有精力旺盛的精神。有时有人会威胁要夺走他的土地，但他从不屈服，也不寻求复仇——他相信和平。他的生活是卓越的，充满意义，在困难时期展现出巨大的勇气。
