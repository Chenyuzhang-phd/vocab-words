故事一：开学第一天

First Day of School
主题：校园生活 | 高亮颜色：■ 黄色
Li Hua is an ardent(热情的) student and the incumbent(现任的) class president, making her classroom the hub(中心) of school activities. She always carries a portfolio(文件夹) of notes and a brochure(小册子) about clubs. At first she worried the new teacher would be arrogant(傲慢的), but that was just a stereotype(刻板印象) — he said arithmetic(算术) is the staple(主要的) foundation of math and repetition(重复) is the best way to learn. During break, she bought stationery(文具) and saw older students intimidate(恐吓) a younger boy, which she thought was shameful(可耻的) and an insult(侮辱) to the school spirit. Her courage was integral(不可或缺的) to school safety, and with each increment(增加) of confidence she grew as a leader. The teacher's wisdom would baffle(使困惑)d her at first, but his kindness was no mere semblance(表象). She used her intelligence(智力) to give an incisive(精辟的) speech at the class meeting.
李华是一个热情的学生，也是现任班长，她的教室是学校活动的中心。她总是带着一个文件夹和一本关于社团的小册子。起初她担心新老师会很傲慢，但这只是一种刻板印象——他说算术是数学的主要基础，重复是最好的学习方法。课间时，她去买文具，看到高年级学生恐吓一个低年级男孩，她觉得这是可耻的，是对学校精神的侮辱。她的勇气对校园安全是不可或缺的，随着信心的增加她成长为更好的领导者。老师的智慧一开始让她困惑，但他的善良不只是表象。她用自己的智力在班会上做了精辟的发言。

故事二：周末大扫除

The Weekend Cleanup
主题：家庭日常 | 高亮颜色：■ 绿色
Saturday morning was always hectic(忙乱的) at Zhang's home — cleanup day! Dad was steadfast(坚定的) and believed good sanitation(卫生) was important for the family. Mom filled a bucket to the brim(边缘) with soapy water and used a garden hose(软管) to wash windows. Xiao Mei found old starch(淀粉) powder, threw it in the trash(垃圾), and tried to squash(压扁) it down, but the brittle(易碎的) bag almost broke. The sink had stagnant(停滞的) water that smelled bad, and the air felt static(静态的) before she opened the window. Xiao Gang cleaned the bathroom until everything was sterile(无菌的), removing hair that started to tangle(缠结) in the drain. He showed great vigilance(警惕) checking every corner, then decided to brew(冲泡) tea for everyone. The family kept the kitchen sanitary(卫生的) so food would not perish(腐烂) easily.
星期六早上在张家总是忙乱的——今天是大扫除日！爸爸很坚定，相信良好的卫生对家庭很重要。妈妈把水桶装到边缘装满肥皂水，用花园软管冲洗窗户。小梅发现一袋过期的淀粉，扔进垃圾里，试着把它压扁，但易碎的垃圾袋差点破掉。水槽里有发臭的停滞的水，打开窗户前空气感觉是静态的。小刚打扫浴室直到一切都无菌，清除了排水口开始缠结的头发。他表现出极大的警惕检查每个角落，然后决定为大家冲泡一杯茶。一家人保持厨房卫生，这样食物就不容易腐烂。
After the cleanup, Xiao Mei checked the old photo frames and was glad they were still intact(完好无损的) after so many years of use. Dad explained that some values, like family love and trust, are intangible(无形的) but more precious than any object we own. The family sat together, drinking tea, feeling grateful for a clean and warm home.
大扫除结束后，小梅检查了旧相框，很高兴它们用了这么多年依然完好无损。爸爸解释说，有些价值，比如家人之间的爱和信任，是无形的，但比我们拥有的任何物品都珍贵。一家人坐在一起喝茶，为干净温暖的家感到感恩。

故事三：第一份实习

My First Internship
主题：职场新人 | 高亮颜色：■ 蓝色
As a novice(新手) intern, Xiao Wang was ready to commence(开始) his inaugural(首次的) day at work. The company offered a generous incentive(激励) of bonuses and free meals, and from his window he could see the marine(海洋的) bay. The company passed new legislation(法规) about flexible hours, and his boss's inference(推断) was that Xiao Wang had legitimate(合理的) talent for creative work. Sometimes he had insomnia(失眠) from excitement, and his obsession(痴迷) with learning kept him up late. He found his own niche(合适的位置) and loved the linen(亚麻的) curtains and lime(酸橙) green walls. At lunch, he visited a nearby zoo and saw a kangaroo(袋鼠) and an ostrich(鸵鸟). The lavish(奢华的) gift shop had a leopard(豹子) plushie. There was a public outcry(强烈抗议) about the zoo being too small, but the benefits outweigh(超过) the costs. The cafe served palatable(可口的) sandwiches and bone marrow(骨髓) soup. An old observatory(天文台) on a hill seemed to loom(赫然耸现) over the path. The guide gave a lucid(清晰的) explanation of telescopes, and he learned that volunteers are like martyr(烈士)s, while the zoo follows linear(线性的) welfare rules.
作为一名新手实习生，小王准备好开始他的首次工作日了。公司提供丰厚的激励——奖金和免费餐食，从靠窗的办公桌他可以看到海洋的海湾。公司通过了关于弹性工作时间的新法规，老板的推断是小王在创意工作方面有合理的天赋。有时他因为兴奋而失眠，对学习的痴迷让他熬夜。他找到了自己合适的位置，喜欢亚麻的窗帘和酸橙绿的墙壁。午休时，他去了附近的动物园，看到了一只袋鼠和一只鸵鸟。奢华的礼品店有一只豹子毛绒玩具。公众对动物园太小表示强烈抗议，但好处超过了成本。咖啡馆供应可口的三明治和骨髓汤。山上的古老天文台似乎赫然耸现在小路上。向导清晰地解释了望远镜，他了解到志愿者就像烈士，动物园遵循线性的福利规则。
One afternoon, a wealthy aristocrat(贵族) visited the office to donate old family documents. The company museum nearby displayed a small arsenal(军械库) of antique swords, and a guide told the long saga(传奇) of the city's founders. During the visit, staff found a breach(缺口) in the database security, which threw the office into a frenzy(狂热). Managers had to repatriate(遣返) the misplaced files to the correct department, and the board voted to repeal(废除) the outdated data rule. Xiao Wang began to repent(后悔) his careless mistake, while his boss reminded him that fear should not retard(阻碍) progress. The team managed to salvage(抢救) most of the records, and the project's resurrection(复苏) made everyone proud.
一天下午，一位富有的贵族来到办公室，捐赠家族的旧文件。公司附近的博物馆陈列着一个小型军械库的古剑，向导讲述了城市创建者的传奇。参观期间，员工发现数据库安全出现缺口，整个办公室陷入狂热。经理们不得不把放错文件遣返到正确的部门，董事会投票废除过时的数据规则。小王开始后悔自己的粗心错误，老板提醒他恐惧不应阻碍进步。团队设法抢救了大部分记录，项目的复苏让所有人感到骄傲。
The next week, Xiao Wang's team visited a coastal resort(度假胜地) for a training camp. A biology expert gave a lecture on reproductive(生殖的/再生的) health and how animals in the marine sanctuary(避难所) live. He spoke without sarcasm(讽刺), and explained that the saline(含盐的) water there is very clean. In a quiet room, the team worked in seclusion(隔离) to design a new plan. Xiao Wang felt a strange sensation(感觉) of excitement, a rich sensory(感官的) experience from seeing the sea for the first time. He noticed an old saddle(马鞍) in the museum corner, and learned that a rival firm tried to sabotage(蓄意破坏) their project once.
第二周，小王的团队去了一个海边度假胜地参加培训营。一位生物学专家做了关于生殖的/再生的健康的讲座，讲解了海洋避难所里动物的生活。他说话没有讽刺，解释说那里的含盐水非常干净。在一个安静的房间里，团队在隔离中设计新方案。小王感到一种奇怪的兴奋的感觉，第一次看到大海带来了丰富的感官体验。他注意到博物馆角落里有一个旧马鞍，还了解到一家竞争公司曾试图蓄意破坏他们的项目。
At the end of the camp, the teacher told them never to tamper(篡改) with company data. A dishonest employee who had committed treason(叛国) against his own firm carried a heavy social stigma(污名) for years. The teacher said the emotional trauma(创伤) from being fired was hard to heal. A good tactic(战术) in tough times is to stay honest and keep learning. Everyone agreed that trust, once lost, is difficult to rebuild.
培训结束时，老师告诉他们绝不要篡改公司数据。一个不诚实的员工对自己的公司犯下叛国之罪，多年来背负着沉重的社会污名。老师说被解雇带来的情感创伤很难愈合。困难时期的一个好战术是保持诚实、不断学习。大家都同意，信任一旦失去就很难重建。

故事四：郊外徒步

A Hike in the Countryside
主题：周末出游 | 高亮颜色：■ 紫色
Last weekend, Xiao Lin and her friends went hiking in the countryside. They saw many aquatic(水生的) plants by a beautiful lake and spotted arboreal(树栖的) squirrels jumping in the trees. A guide said a carnivore(食肉动物) like a fox lives in these woods. Most animals here are not nocturnal(夜间活动的) — they are active during the day. Every predator(捕食者) plays an important role in nature, while rabbits and deer are common prey(猎物) for larger animals. They also saw a small reptile(爬行动物) — a lizard sunning on a rock. A mouse is a type of rodent(啮齿动物) that lives in fields, and a crow is a scavenger(食腐动物) that eats whatever it finds. The dense vegetation(植被) in the forest provides homes and food for all these creatures.
上周末，小林和朋友们去郊外徒步。他们看到一个美丽的湖边生长着许多水生的植物，还发现树栖的松鼠在树上跳来跳去。一位向导说，像狐狸这样的食肉动物生活在这片树林里。这里的大多数动物都不是夜间活动的——它们白天活动。每一个捕食者都在自然界中扮演着重要的角色，而兔子和鹿是大型动物常见的猎物。他们还看到了一只小型爬行动物——一只在岩石上晒太阳的蜥蜴。老鼠是一种生活在田野里的啮齿动物，乌鸦是一种食腐动物，吃它能找到的任何东西。森林里茂密的植被为所有这些生物提供了家园和食物。
The hike was arduous(艰巨的) but fun, and they had to traverse(穿越) a dry, arid(干旱的) hill to reach the top. At the intersection(交叉路口) of two trails, they saw a shy badger(獾) run into the bushes. A guide warned that leaving the path could backfire(适得其反), since getting lost in the woods is dangerous. They noticed a fox stalk(潜行跟踪) a rabbit from behind the trees, very quiet and careful. Later, a sudden storm came, and a farmer helped them bail(保释/脱困) out when their tent flooded with water.
这次徒步很艰巨但很有趣，他们不得不穿越一座干旱的山丘才能到达山顶。在两条小径的交叉路口，他们看到一只害羞的獾跑进灌木丛。向导警告说离开小路可能适得其反，因为在树林里迷路很危险。他们注意到一只狐狸从树后潜行跟踪一只兔子，非常安静小心。后来突然来了暴风雨，他们的帐篷进了水，一位农夫帮他们脱困。

故事五：超市大采购

A Day at the Supermarket
主题：购物日记 | 高亮颜色：■ 橙色
On Saturday, Xiao Li went to the supermarket where ads would allude(暗示) to great deals as she liked to amble(漫步) through the aisles, spotting cookies Aloft(在高处) on the top shelf. The alpine(高山的) yogurt came from a farm at high altitude(海拔), and the amenable(通情达理的) manager greeted everyone warmly. An old lady with amnesia(健忘症) who was bilingual(双语的) asked for help, and the fruit section had amber(琥珀色的) peaches and aluminum(铝制的) cans. An ambiguous(模棱两可的) price label was a blunder(错误) by the staff, and she felt ambivalent(矛盾的) about buying snacks. The bread section sold analogue(类似物)s of traditional cakes, and candy came in amorphous(无定形的) shapes that were truly bizarre(奇异的). She liked studying the anatomy(构造) of fruits, heard anecdotal(传闻的) ghost stories, and saw a colossal(巨大的) display of bleach(漂白剂). She bought a new blouse(女式衬衫), the scale needed calibration(校准), and outside a vendor(小贩) sold flowers while a pedestrian(行人) with a pendant(吊坠) walked by. Stealing is a sin(罪过), and a sinister(阴险的) man made her uneasy. People say the old town was once full of anarchy(无政府状态), the fire alarm sounded like a siren(警报器), and without food people would die of starvation(饥饿). The store had high stature(声望) and followed every local statute(法规); long lines could torment(折磨) shoppers but self-checkout helps unravel(解决) the problem. A proponent(支持者) of healthy eating stood by the vegetables, the store had to purge(清除) old food weekly, and the jewelry counter had beautiful quartz(石英) stones. Theft was rampant(猖獗的) last year but cameras helped rectify(纠正) it, and during the economic upheaval(动荡) the store changed a lot. Fresh varnish(清漆) made floors shine, the manager worked to vindicate(证明...正确) his price cuts, and the pet section had books about viper(毒蛇) snakes. Business was at its zenith(顶峰) during holidays; the owner was a maverick(特立独行者) whose favorite maxim(格言) was "the customer is always right." Monthly Mediation(调解) occurred between staff and management, the history section had medieval(中世纪的) books, and some customers liked to meditate(冥想) in the quiet corner. Staff would nominate(提名) the best worker each month, the obstinate(固执的) bakery chef never changed recipes, and the flower shop had venomous(有毒的)-looking plants that were harmless.
星期六，小李去超市购物，广告会暗示有超值优惠，她喜欢在过道里漫步，在高处的顶层货架上发现了饼干。高山酸奶来自高海拔的农场，通情达理的店长热情地迎接顾客。一位患健忘症的双语老太太请求帮助，水果区有琥珀色的桃子和铝制的果汁罐。模棱两可的价格标签是员工的错误，她对买零食感到矛盾。面包区出售传统糕点的类似物，糖果有无定形的形状，看起来真的很奇异。她喜欢研究水果的构造，听到过传闻的鬼故事，还看到了一个巨大的漂白剂展示架。她买了一件新的女式衬衫，秤需要校准，外面一个小贩在卖花，一个戴着吊坠的行人走过。偷窃是罪过，一个阴险的男人让她不安。人们说老城区曾经充满无政府状态，火警听起来像警报器，没有食物人们会死于饥饿。这家店声望很高，遵守每项当地法规；长队会折磨购物者但自助结账有助于解决问题。一位健康饮食的支持者站在蔬菜旁，商店每周清除过期食品，珠宝柜台有美丽的石英。去年盗窃猖獗但摄像头帮助纠正了情况，经济动荡期间商店变化很大。新的清漆让地板闪亮，经理努力证明降价是正确的，宠物区有关于毒蛇的书。节日期间生意处于顶峰；店主是个特立独行者，最喜欢的格言是"顾客永远是对的"。每月员工和管理层之间有调解，历史区有中世纪的书，一些顾客喜欢在安静的角落冥想。员工每月提名最佳员工，固执的面包师从不改变食谱，花店有看起来有毒但其实无害的植物。
In the following weeks, Xiao Li, an aspiring(有抱负的) young worker, tried to assimilate(吸收) new skills to become a real asset(资产) to her team. Her boss was astute(精明的) and would astound(使震惊) everyone with quick decisions, and small acts of courtesy(礼貌) always avail(有益于) the team's morale(士气). She felt anticipation(期待) before each meeting and would blush(脸红) when praised. Her grandma had arthritis(关节炎) and sometimes felt pain in her abdomen(腹部), so Xiao Li looked for an alternative(替代方案) treatment to help. The doctor's abominable(可憎的) handwriting caused some ambiguity(模棱两可), but a cozy(舒适的) rest and a soft lullaby(摇篮曲) helped grandma sleep well. Unfortunately, her cousin's absenteeism(缺勤) at school became a problem.
接下来的几周里，有抱负的年轻员工小李努力吸收新技能，想成为团队真正的资产。她的老板很精明，快速的决定使所有人震惊，小小的礼貌之举总是有益于团队士气。每次开会前她都充满期待，被表扬时会脸红。她的奶奶患有关节炎，有时腹部会疼，所以小李寻找替代方案来帮助。医生可憎的字迹造成了一些模棱两可，但舒适的休息和柔和的摇篮曲让奶奶睡得很好。不幸的是，她表弟的缺勤成了一个问题。
One weekend, Xiao Li went on a short cruise(巡游) on a small cruiser(游艇) with her friends. A straightforward(直截了当的) young man told her not to covet(垂涎) trivial(琐碎的) things, and his motto(格言) was "keep life simple." They saw a beautiful ornament(装饰品) at a local auction(拍卖), but an audacious(大胆的) bidder offered a lucrative(有利可图的) price. At dinner, the crisp(脆的) cracker(饼干)s were served on crude(粗糙的) wooden plates, and the food was far from monotonous(单调的). A malicious(恶意的) rumor tried to malign(诽谤) the boat owner, but no one believed it. Her brother's courtship(求偶) with his girlfriend won him a trophy(奖杯) at the town's contest.
一个周末，小李和朋友坐小型游艇进行了一次短途巡游。一个直截了当的年轻人告诉她不要垂涎琐碎的东西，他的格言是"让生活简单"。他们在当地拍卖上看到一个漂亮的装饰品，但一个大胆的竞价者报出了有利可图的价格。晚餐时，脆的饼干放在粗糙的木盘上，食物一点也不单调。一个恶意的谣言试图诽谤船主，但没人相信。她哥哥和女朋友的求偶在镇上的比赛中赢得了奖杯。
At the history museum, Xiao Li saw an assemblage(聚集) of old artillery(炮兵) and an exhibit about a plot to assassinate(暗杀) a king, which was a rare occurrence(事件). A guide said seeking asylum(庇护) is a basic human right, and people in mourning(哀悼) wore black. She saw an asymmetrical(不对称的) shape on a vase and a strange auger(钻孔器) tool used for mining ore(矿石) from the earth. The museum did an audit(审计) every year, and actors held an audition(试镜/测试) for a play about the aurora(极光/光彩) in the sky. She learned about the orbit(轨道) of planets around the sun, that loud noises were audible(听得见的) far away, and that an orthodox(正统的) tradition should not obstruct(阻碍) new ideas.
在历史博物馆，小李看到了一个炮兵的聚集，还有一个关于暗杀国王的阴谋的展览，这是一件罕见的事件。向导说寻求庇护是基本人权，哀悼的人们穿着黑色。她看到了花瓶上一个不对称的形状和一个奇怪的钻孔器，用来从地下开采矿石。博物馆每年进行审计，演员们为一部关于天空极光/光彩的戏剧进行试镜/测试。她了解到行星绕太阳的轨道运行，巨大的噪音在远处也听得见，正统的传统不应阻碍新思想。
The museum's credibility(可信度) was well known — every exhibit was carefully researched and verified by experts.
博物馆的可信度是众所周知的——每一件展品都经过专家的仔细研究和验证。

故事六：学做蛋糕

Learning to Bake a Cake
主题：美食厨房 | 高亮颜色：■ 青色

Xiao Ming was preparing for the college entrance exam. As a novice(新手) at big exams, he was diligent(勤勉的) and meticulous(细致的) in his studies. His desk was in the loft(阁楼) — quiet and spacious(宽敞的), and he made a pledge(承诺) to work hard using a special methodology(方法论). The city view outside was vibrant(生机勃勃的) and he felt resilient(有韧性的) even on tough days. In history class, he learned about the renaissance(复兴) — a time of magnificent(壮丽的) art and great thinkers. Great leaders showed foresight(远见), and he loved the brevity(简洁) of ancient poems and admired candid(率直的) writers. The poignant(深刻的) emotion in good literature touched him, and he enjoyed animated(活泼的) class discussions. In science, he learned about the spectrum(光谱) of light and our galaxy(星系), the Milky Way. He dreamed of becoming a stellar(一流的) scientist, studied how plants release pollen(花粉) and nature's amazing adaptation(适应) to different environments. Some animals have innate(天生的) abilities that help them survive. In chemistry, he learned about solvent(溶剂)s and noxious(有害的) gases, and how the immune(免疫的) system fights disease. Sometimes his scores hit a plateau(平稳期), but he knew progress is not always linear(线性的). He tried new methods to stabilise(使稳定) his grades and used qualitative(定性的) analysis to understand mistakes. He wanted to qualify(有资格) for the best university, and break time felt ephemeral(短暂的) but precious. He felt abashed(窘迫的) thinking about how much he still had to learn, stress sometimes caused indigestion(消化不良), and the distress(痛苦) of exam pressure was hard to bear. His mom would soothe(安慰) him with warm soup and kind words. He refused to let apathy(冷漠) take over and would never forsake(放弃) his dreams or let his spirit wither(凋谢). He knew stress could inhibit(抑制) clear thinking, smartphones have become ubiquitous(无处不在的), and he learned about inflation(通货膨胀) and the simple hierarchy(等级制度) of the school system. He was ingenious(机灵的) at finding new ways to solve problems, and there was often a discrepancy(差异) between his practice and real exam scores. After long study sessions, he liked to walk in the tranquil(宁静的) park nearby. The museum had fossil(化石) exhibits, he loved eating lobster(龙虾) on hot days, and he watched dolphin(海豚) shows on TV. He visited grandma in a village — her house had a dome(圆屋顶)-shaped roof and lotus(荷花) flowers in the pond. He read about a veteran(老兵) soldier who was a visionary(有远见的) leader — people venerate(崇敬)d him as a hero. He learned that small problems can escalate(升级) if not dealt with early, new inventions proliferate(激增) every year, and he liked small gadget(小器械)s with magnetic(有磁性的) parts. Insulate(绝缘的)d wires keep electricity safe, he often faced a dilemma(困境) — study math or English first, and hard work constitute(构成)s the key to success. Every facet(方面) of learning interested him, human frailty(脆弱) means everyone makes mistakes, and Sporadic(零星的) rain showers sometimes interrupted his study. He was determined to rectify(纠正) his weak subjects and would meditate(冥想) for ten minutes daily to relax his mind.
